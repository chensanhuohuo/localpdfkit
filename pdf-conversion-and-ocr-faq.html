PDF Conversion and OCR FAQ

Conversion and OCR results are shaped by the source file. These answers explain what to expect and how to troubleshoot common document extraction problems.

Why does PDF to Word formatting change?

PDFs describe final page appearance, while Word files need an editable structure. Complex layouts, tables, columns, and custom fonts can shift during that reconstruction.

Why is OCR less accurate on some files?

OCR depends heavily on scan quality, contrast, page rotation, language selection, and whether the original text is clear enough to recognize.

Should I OCR a document before converting it?

If the PDF is just scanned images with no real text layer, OCR is often the right first step before attempting editable reuse.

What is the best way to check the result?

Review headings, tables, page breaks, footnotes, and small text. Those areas reveal conversion issues faster than a quick glance at the first page.
