Cookie Policy

Last Updated: April 12, 2026

1. Scope of This Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how LocalPDFKit uses cookies, local storage, and similar browser technologies when you visit or use localpdfkit.com.

2. Essential Browser Storage

LocalPDFKit uses certain browser-side storage features to make the site work properly. These may include remembering interface preferences, layout settings, tool options, or other non-account settings needed for the site to function across visits.

These technologies are typically stored in your browser rather than on our servers and may include local storage or similar mechanisms instead of traditional cookies.

3. Analytics and Measurement

We may use privacy-focused measurement tools to understand broad site usage, such as which pages are visited most often or how users reach our site. Where such tools are enabled, we aim to minimize the collection of personal data and avoid unnecessary tracking.

If analytics settings change in a way that materially affects this policy, we will update this page.

4. Optional Third-Party Services

Some optional features may rely on third-party resources such as OCR language data, WebAssembly files, or measurement tools, depending on how the site is configured.

If those integrations change in a way that affects browser storage or privacy expectations, we will update this page with clearer details.

5. Third-Party Requests

Some optional features may load assets from configured third-party locations, such as OCR language files, WebAssembly modules, or analytics scripts. Those services may have their own policies and storage behavior. We encourage you to review the policies of any third-party service you interact with through the site.

6. Managing Your Preferences

Most browsers allow you to block or clear cookies and local storage. Doing so may reset your preferences or affect how parts of the site behave, including interface settings and saved tool options.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect technical or legal changes. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about cookies or browser storage on LocalPDFKit, contact contact@localpdfkit.com.
