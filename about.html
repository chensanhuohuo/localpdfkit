Our Mission

To provide the most comprehensive PDF toolbox that respects your privacy and avoids unnecessary accounts or uploads. We believe essential document tools should be accessible to everyone, everywhere, without unnecessary barriers.

Our Core Philosophy

Privacy First. Always.

In an era where data is a commodity, we take a different approach. All processing for LocalPDFKit tools happens locally in your browser. This means your files never touch our servers, we never see your documents, and we don't track what you do. Your documents remain completely and unequivocally private. It's not just a feature; it's our foundation.

Why LocalPDFKit?

Built for Speed

No waiting for uploads or downloads to a server. By processing files directly in your browser using modern web technologies like WebAssembly, we offer unparalleled speed for all our tools.

Free to Use

Core tools are available without an account, subscription, or hidden upload workflow. We aim to keep common PDF tasks simple, transparent, and broadly accessible.

No Account Required

Start using any tool immediately. We don't need your email, a password, or any personal information. Your workflow should be frictionless and anonymous.

Open Source Route

LocalPDFKit is operated on an AGPL-compliant open-source route. We publish source-code and attribution information so users can understand the upstream software, our modifications, and the LocalPDFKit brand boundary.

Read the source code notice View the GitHub repository

Built From Open Source, Operated as an Independent Brand

LocalPDFKit is an independent PDF tools website at localpdfkit.com. It is not the official website or support channel for BentoPDF or any other upstream project.

Our deployment is based on open-source software and maintained under an AGPL route. Upstream licenses continue to apply to the relevant code and components, while LocalPDFKit brand assets and original editorial content remain distinct.

You can read more on our Licensing and Source Code pages, or review the public repository at github.com/chensanhuohuo/localpdfkit.
