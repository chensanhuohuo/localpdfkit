Archive Guide

How to Prepare a PDF for Archiving

Archive-ready PDFs usually need more than a simple save. You may need to review metadata, embedded fonts, transparency, attachments, and the target archival standard before storing a file long term.

Recommended Workflow

  1. Review the source PDF for visible issues, missing fonts, or damaged pages.
  2. Remove metadata or other unnecessary extras if your archive policy requires it.
  3. Convert to the PDF/A level that matches your workflow.
  4. Open the output and review a few pages before storing it permanently.

When PDF/A Helps

PDF/A is often used for document retention, records management, and archive workflows where portability and long-term readability matter more than flexible editing.

What to Check After Conversion

Review page appearance, text readability, attachments, and any elements that could change during flattening or archival cleanup.
