Image Conversion Tutorial

How to Convert Images to PDF

Image-to-PDF conversion is useful for scans, receipts, forms, and photo-based records. The most important things to control are page order, page orientation, and image quality.

Basic Workflow

  1. Open the JPG to PDF tool.
  2. Add the images in the order you want them to appear.
  3. Choose a practical quality level for sharing or archiving.
  4. Generate the PDF and review each page before sending it.

When Lower Quality Helps

If the PDF is mainly for email, upload forms, or internal review, a balanced or lower image setting can keep the file manageable.

When Higher Quality Helps

Use higher quality for receipts with small print, scanned contracts, or records where fine details must remain readable after zooming in.
