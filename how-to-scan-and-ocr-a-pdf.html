How to Scan and OCR a PDF

OCR turns scanned pages into searchable text. The result depends on source quality, language selection, and whether the original pages are clean enough for text recognition.

Recommended Workflow

  1. Open the OCR PDF tool.
  2. Upload a scanned PDF with readable page images.
  3. Select the document languages as accurately as possible.
  4. Use advanced settings only when the default pass is not good enough.
  5. Review the extracted text and download the searchable PDF.

What Improves OCR

  • Straight scans with good contrast and minimal blur
  • Correct language selection
  • Higher source resolution on text-heavy pages
  • Cleaner originals without heavy marks across the text

What Often Hurts OCR

  • Low-resolution photos of documents
  • Mixed languages that are not selected
  • Skewed pages, dark shadows, or handwritten notes over text
  • Decorative fonts and dense tables in the original scan

Searchable PDF vs Extracted Text

A searchable PDF keeps the original page appearance and adds a text layer underneath. Extracted text is better when you want to copy content into notes, spreadsheets, or another editor.
