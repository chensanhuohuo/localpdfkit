How to Recover a Damaged PDF

Damaged PDFs can fail in different ways: blank pages, broken fonts, missing images, or files that refuse to open at all. Recovery is often about restoring readability first, then checking what still needs manual review.

Recovery Workflow

  1. Try opening the original file once to confirm the failure symptom.
  2. Run the file through the Repair PDF tool.
  3. Open the repaired file and review every critical page.
  4. If the file still has issues, try extracting readable pages or images separately.

Common Signs of Damage

  • The PDF will not open at all
  • Pages render blank or partially blank
  • Images disappear or appear corrupted
  • Text looks incomplete, scrambled, or missing

Important Review Step

A repaired file may become readable again without being identical to the original. Always compare key pages before using it for business, legal, or archive purposes.
