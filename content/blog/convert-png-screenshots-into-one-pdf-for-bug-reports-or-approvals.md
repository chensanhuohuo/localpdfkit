PNG screenshots are useful, but they become hard to manage once you have ten or twenty of them. That is where a single PDF becomes easier to review, easier to forward, and easier to archive.

This is especially practical for bug reports, approval trails, UI reviews, receipts, and internal documentation.

## Good situations for PNG to PDF

Use [PNG to PDF](/png-to-pdf) when you need to turn screenshots into one file for:

- bug reports
- release notes
- design approvals
- process documentation
- support escalations

One PDF is often easier for another person to read in sequence than a folder full of images.

## Step 1: Put the screenshots in the order you want reviewed

Before conversion, name or arrange the images in the same order the story should be read.

Examples:

- before, during, after
- step 1, step 2, step 3
- issue, evidence, expected result

The conversion is more useful when the screenshot order already makes sense.

## Step 2: Convert the PNG files into one PDF

Open [PNG to PDF](/png-to-pdf) and add the screenshots you want in the final packet. If the input includes other image types too, [Image to PDF](/image-to-pdf) may be the more flexible route.

## Step 3: Organize the final document if needed

After conversion, use [Organize PDF](/organize-pdf) if you want to reorder pages, remove duplicates, or tighten the flow before sharing.

This is especially helpful when the screenshots came from multiple people or devices.

## Step 4: Compress the result for email or ticket systems

Screenshot-heavy PDFs can become larger than expected. If the final file needs to go into email or a ticketing tool, [Compress PDF](/compress-pdf) is a useful final step.

## Why one PDF often works better than many images

### It creates a clearer narrative

Reviewers can follow the issue page by page.

### It reduces attachment clutter

One file is easier to upload and forward.

### It is easier to archive

A single document is simpler to label and keep.

## Common mistakes

### Adding screenshots in random order

That forces the reviewer to rebuild the timeline themselves.

### Forgetting to remove duplicate captures

Too much repetition makes the file longer without adding evidence.

### Sending an oversized screenshot pack

Compress after the order is final.
