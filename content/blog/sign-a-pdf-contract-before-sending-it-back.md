When a contract or approval form arrives as a PDF, printing it, signing it by hand, and scanning it again is often the slowest possible route. If the workflow allows it, signing the PDF directly is usually cleaner and faster.

This is one of the most practical document tasks for contracts, offer letters, onboarding forms, and vendor paperwork.

## When to sign a PDF directly

Use [Sign PDF](/sign-pdf) when:

- a client sent a contract for signature
- an employer sent an offer letter or onboarding packet
- a vendor approval form needs your signature
- a landlord, school, or service provider expects a signed PDF back

The point is to return a completed document without unnecessary printing and rescanning.

## Step 1: Review the document before signing

Before you sign anything, confirm:

- the latest version is in front of you
- names, dates, and amounts are correct
- signature fields are in the right places
- no pages are missing

Signing the wrong version is harder to unwind than delaying for one more review.

## Step 2: Add the signature to the PDF

Open [Sign PDF](/sign-pdf) and place the signature where the document expects it. If there are initials, dates, or multiple signature areas, complete them all before exporting the final copy.

The goal is to produce a finished file that another person can review immediately.

## Step 3: Flatten the signed file if the workflow needs stability

If the signed PDF should behave like a final document rather than an editable form, [Flatten PDF](/flatten-pdf) can help lock the visible content into place after completion.

This is especially helpful when the file is going to another system, another team, or a portal that does not handle interactive layers well.

## Step 4: Review privacy and size before sending

If the signed document is going outside your organization, check [Remove Metadata](/remove-metadata). If the file is too large for email or upload, use [Compress PDF](/compress-pdf) at the end.

## Why this workflow is better than printing and rescanning

### It keeps the file cleaner

Printing and rescanning often lowers readability.

### It is faster

There is less back-and-forth between devices.

### It usually preserves the document structure better

That matters for forms, page order, and reviewer experience.

## Common mistakes

### Signing before reviewing the final version

Always check the content first.

### Sending the signed copy without opening it once

Make sure the signature is visible and the pages still look right.

### Compressing or editing the file in the middle of the signature workflow

Finish the signature first, then do any share-prep cleanup.
