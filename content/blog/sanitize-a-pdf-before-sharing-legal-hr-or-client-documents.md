Before a PDF leaves your team, it is easy to focus only on the visible pages. But PDFs can also carry metadata, annotations, scripts, attachments, form data, and other hidden details.

[Sanitize PDF](/sanitize-pdf) is for creating a cleaner sharing copy when the document is headed to a client, vendor, HR process, legal review, or public download.

## When sanitizing makes sense

Consider sanitizing when you share:

- legal drafts
- HR forms
- client deliverables
- procurement documents
- public templates
- policy packets
- redacted review copies

The goal is not to panic about every file. The goal is to avoid sending unnecessary document baggage.

## Step 1: Finish the visible document first

Sanitize after the document is final. If you edit, annotate, merge, or compress after sanitizing, new metadata or content may be created again.

The better order is:

1. finish editing
2. remove or flatten comments
3. delete unwanted pages
4. sanitize the sharing copy
5. review the final file

## Step 2: Decide whether metadata removal is enough

If your only concern is document properties like author, title, software, and timestamps, [Remove Metadata](/remove-metadata) may be enough.

Use full sanitization when you want broader cleanup beyond metadata.

## Step 3: Remove annotations when comments should not travel

Review comments, highlights, links, and other markup before sending the file. If comments should not leave the team, use [Remove Annotations](/remove-annotations) or include annotation cleanup in your sanitization workflow.

For files where visible markup should stay but become fixed, [Flatten PDF](/flatten-pdf) can help.

## Step 4: Keep an original copy

Sanitization is a final-sharing step. Keep the original working file separately in case you need to edit, audit, or resend a different version later.

## Common mistakes

### Sanitizing too early

Edits made afterward can create fresh metadata.

### Assuming redaction and sanitization are the same

Redaction handles visible sensitive content. Sanitization handles hidden or extra document data.

### Sending the working copy instead of the clean copy

Name the sanitized version clearly so it is easy to find.

If you want to understand what the file contains before cleanup, start with [View Metadata](/view-metadata) and then decide whether metadata removal or full sanitization is the right step.
